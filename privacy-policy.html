3. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used for the following purposes:

  1. BMI Calculation: Determine body mass index and health classification
  2. Personalize Experience: Provide recommendations tailored to your goals
  3. Track Progress: Store history and create development charts
  4. Improve Services: Analyze usage trends to enhance quality
  5. Send Notifications: Weight update reminders, health tips (if consented)
  6. Customer Support: Resolve technical issues and support requests
  7. Security: Prevent fraud and service abuse
⚠️ Important Note: We will NEVER sell your personal information to third parties. Your health data is completely confidential and only used to serve you better.

We implement multiple security measures to protect your information:

  • Security Updates: Regular updates and security patches
